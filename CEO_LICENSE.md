The CEO is authorized to ensure compliance with:

- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** Data protection and privacy
- **MiFID II:** Markets in Financial Instruments Directive

- **PSD2:** Payment Services Directive 2
- **eIDAS:** Electronic Identification and Trust Services
- **DORA:** Digital Operational Resilience Act
- **NIS2:** Network and Information Security Directive

### 2.2 National Regulations

Compliance with laws in all EU member states where the Service operates:

- Financial services regulations
- Consumer protection laws
- Data protection laws
- Cybersecurity requirements
- Tax regulations
- Employment laws

The CEO is authorized to:

The CEO is authorized to:

The CEO is authorized to:

The CEO is authorized to:

- File regulatory reports with EU authorities

The CEO's authority is limited to:

- Legal and ethical business practices

- Engage in illegal activities
