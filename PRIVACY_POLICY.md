# Privacy Policy - AgentPay Wallet

**Effective Date:** May 23, 2026  
**Last Updated:** May 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction

AgentPay Wallet ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (the "Service").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information:** Email, username, password, profile information
- **Wallet Information:** Ethereum addresses, transaction history
- **Bank Information:** IBAN, bank account details (encrypted)
- **Communication:** Messages, support tickets, feedback
- **Device Information:** Device ID, operating system, app version

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Usage Data:** Features used, transaction types, time spent
- **Device Data:** IP address, device type, browser type
- **Location Data:** Approximate location (only with permission)
- **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** Session tokens, preferences

### 2.3 Third-Party Information

- **Blockchain Data:** Public blockchain transactions (non-personal)
- **Payment Processors:** Transaction confirmations
- **Analytics Providers:** Aggregated usage statistics

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

- **Service Provision:** Operating and maintaining the wallet
- **Transaction Processing:** Executing swaps, transfers, and payments
- **Security:** Fraud detection, account protection
- **Compliance:** KYC/AML requirements, regulatory compliance
- **Communication:** Support, updates, notifications
- **Analytics:** Improving service quality
- **Legal Obligations:** Responding to legal requests

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account Data:** Retained while account is active + 3 years after deletion
- **Transaction Data:** Retained for 7 years (regulatory requirement)
- **Personal Data:** Deleted upon request (except where legally required)
- **Backup Data:** Retained for 30 days after deletion

## 5. Data Security

We implement:

- **Encryption:** AES-256 for sensitive data
- **Secure Storage:** Encrypted local storage on device
- **Biometric Authentication:** Fingerprint/Face ID support
- **PIN Protection:** Optional PIN-based access control
- **HTTPS/TLS:** Encrypted data transmission
- **Regular Audits:** Security assessments and penetration testing

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate information
- **Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
- **Restriction:** Limit how we use your data
- **Portability:** Receive your data in a portable format
- **Objection:** Opt-out of certain processing
- **Withdraw Consent:** Revoke consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@agentpay.app

## 7. Data Sharing

We share data with:

- **Service Providers:** Payment processors, analytics providers (under contracts)
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law or court order
- **Business Partners:** Only with explicit consent
- **Blockchain:** Public wallet addresses (non-personal)

We **do not** sell your personal data to third parties.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the EU. We ensure adequate safeguards through:

- **Standard Contractual Clauses:** EU-approved data transfer mechanisms
- **Privacy Shield:** Where applicable
- **Adequacy Decisions:** Transfers to adequate jurisdictions

## 9. Cookies and Tracking

We use:

- **Session Cookies:** For authentication and security
- **Preference Cookies:** To remember your settings
- **Analytics Cookies:** To understand usage patterns

You can disable cookies in your device settings.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover we have collected data from a child, we will delete it immediately.

## 11. Third-Party Links
